Something not working?

Write to us and a person answers, usually within a day. If the clock did something strange, say which format you were running and it will be a much shorter conversation.

Asked often

Does Vedy count my reps?

No. Vedy is a timer and a log. Reps are yours to add on the summary if you counted them, and a session is complete without them.

My streak broke on a rest day

Rest days only count once they are set. Pick them in Settings, or pause the streak entirely while you are away.

Can I use different numbers each round?

Yes, with MIX. Repeating a timer runs the same block several times; MIX chains different blocks into one session.

Where do progress photos live?

On your device, in Vedy under You. They are not uploaded and nobody else can open them. Delete them whenever you like.

How much does Vedy cost?

Nothing. There are no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.
